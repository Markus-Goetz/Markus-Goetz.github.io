Seminar for Bachelor and Master students at TUM Campus Heilbronn, offered in Winter Semester 2026/2027, focusing on machine learning methods applied to computer vision problems, 3 ECTS points.

Students engage with current research papers and methods at the intersection of machine learning and computer vision, developing skills in critical reading, presentation, and independent exploration of the literature.
