Lecture for Bachelor students at TUM Campus Heilbronn, offered in Winter Semester 2026/2027, introducing the foundations of machine learning as the third course in the Data Science lecture series.

Topics covered include supervised and unsupervised learning, model evaluation, and core algorithms underlying modern machine learning systems, providing students with the conceptual and practical grounding needed for further study in data science and AI.
