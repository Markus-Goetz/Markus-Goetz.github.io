Accurate heliostat alignment is critical for the efficiency of concentrating solar power tower plants, but calibration is traditionally slow and labor-intensive. This work introduces a differentiable ray tracing approach that automatically learns heliostat geometries and alignment parameters directly from in-situ operational measurements, enabling scalable, self-calibrating metrology for solar tower plants.
