Limited access to real-world operational data has long hindered research on concentrating solar power (CSP) tower plants. We introduce PAINT, a FAIR (findable, accessible, interoperable and reusable) open-access database compiling 849 GB of multi-year operational data from the Jülich solar tower plant, including heliostat properties, calibration and deflectometry measurements, and fine-grained weather data. Organized using the SpatioTemporal Asset Catalog specification, PAINT is designed to support digital twins, AI-based calibration methods, predictive maintenance, and improved solar flux prediction across the CSP research community.
