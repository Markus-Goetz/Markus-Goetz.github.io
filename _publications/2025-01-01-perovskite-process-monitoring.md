Scaling up perovskite solar cell manufacturing requires reliable, real-time insight into the thin-film fabrication process. This paper introduces a deep learning approach that augments in-situ process monitoring, extracting predictive signals directly from luminescence data recorded during fabrication. The resulting models help identify process irregularities early and support more robust, data-driven scale-up of perovskite photovoltaics production.
