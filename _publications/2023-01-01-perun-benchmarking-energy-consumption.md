Understanding and reducing the energy footprint of high-performance computing applications is essential for sustainable AI and scientific computing. We introduce perun, an open-source tool for benchmarking the energy consumption of parallel and distributed applications across CPUs, GPUs, and full HPC nodes with minimal overhead. Perun provides fine-grained, reproducible energy measurements that support both individual researchers and larger-scale energy-efficiency studies.
