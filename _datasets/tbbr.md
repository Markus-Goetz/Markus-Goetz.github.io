TBBR (Thermal Bridges on Building Rooftops) is a hyperspectral drone imagery dataset of Karlsruhe, Germany, combining RGB, thermal, and height channels with manual annotations of thermal bridges on building rooftops. It supports the development and benchmarking of deep learning methods for automated thermal bridge detection from aerial imagery. The dataset has 6 contributors.
